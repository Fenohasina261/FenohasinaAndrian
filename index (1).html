Case 02 — Vehicle Rental

Direct bookings & customer capture without intermediaries

SEO & Local Ads

Focused on the tourism rental sector, this case demonstrates the power of a targeted search strategy from the moment travel intent begins. The Challenge

Case 03 — Education & Training Institution

Analytics overhaul & data-driven student recruitment

Analytics & Data SEO

This case illustrates the highly analytical side of digital marketing. By deploying custom tracking (GA4 / GTM) and filtering out internal noise, leadership was able to manage student recruitment with real metrics on applicants' geographic origin and interest in each program.

The Challenge

The institution had no clear view of where its applicants came from (cities, countries) or their real interest in its programs, and its data was distorted by daily visits from its own staff.

The Approach

Built a complete measurement and filtering system (GA4 / GTM) to isolate internal traffic and give leadership a reliable dashboard focused on application generation (SEO + behavior).

Applicant Origin & Data Filtering
Average Engagement by Step (Minutes)
Key Takeaways
  • Decisions based on real data: recommended exclusion of the 64 internal staff visits to count only genuine applicants.
  • High-performing SEO acquisition: 160 new users (60%+ of traffic) from Google, organically.
  • Profitable application funnel: 2 min 16s of attention on program pages and 1 min 43s on the international application file.
